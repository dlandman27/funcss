About

Small websites. Endless distractions.

Random Sites On the Web is a collection of tiny, single-purpose websites. Each one does exactly one thing: pop some bubble wrap, draw a perfect circle, grow a garden, spin a slot machine, read a random Wikipedia article, or just stare at something nice for a minute.

The idea comes from the old internet, when you could stumble onto a strange little site that someone made just because they thought it would be fun. There’s no feed, no account, and no point. Press “Surprise Me!” on the homepage and see where you end up, or browse the full catalog of games, art, tools, and toys.

Who makes it

Every site is designed and built by Dylan Landman, a software engineer who likes making small things on the web. New toys are added regularly.
